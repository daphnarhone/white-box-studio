Frequently asked questions

Everything worth knowing before you start.

We've gathered the questions we're asked most about working with the studio, from process to service area. Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll be glad to help.

What services do you offer?

We're a studio for handmade architectural finishes, decorative plaster, sculpture, gypsum panels, iron-look coating, wall murals, decorative concrete, capitals and ornaments, sculptural tables, and display accessories.

Is the work bespoke?

Yes. Almost everything we make is designed and produced specifically for the space, the client, and the intent.

Do you work with architects and interior designers?

Absolutely. We co-design alongside architects, designers, and end clients, and handle full on-site installation.

How do I start a project?

Send us reference images, the project type, timeline, and space details by WhatsApp or email, and we'll come back with a direction and a quote.

Do you install on site?

Yes, we carry out full installation at the client's site.

When should I bring you in?

As early as possible, involving us at the planning stage ensures all required infrastructure is planned in advance.

Do you work in commercial and private spaces?

Yes, both, homes, restaurants, hotels, retail, and offices.

Do you work only in Israel?

Yes, we currently take on projects in Israel only.

How much does a project cost?

Each project is priced individually by scope, materials, and complexity. We provide a quote once we understand the project.

When was the studio established?

The studio was founded in 2005, with two decades of handmade architectural finishes.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us about it, and we'll come back with a direction.
